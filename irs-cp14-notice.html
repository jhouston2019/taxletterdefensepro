IRS CP14 Notice — Balance Due Explained

What This Letter Is

A CP14 notice is typically the first bill the IRS sends when they believe you owe taxes. It shows the amount due, including penalties and interest, and requests payment by a specific date.

Many people receive this notice even when the balance is the result of a filing error, timing issue, or missing information.

What Happens If You Ignore It

  • Additional penalties and interest may accrue
  • Follow-up collection notices may be issued
  • The balance can escalate if unaddressed
